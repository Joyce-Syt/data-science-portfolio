**Research Question**: How do environmental sensor metrics correlate with urban emergency incident frequency?

![Emergency Trend Visualization](trend_plot.png)
The visualisation shows emergency events peak during evening hours and rise when temperature and air quality degrade. Correlation analysis indicates environmental conditions are associated with emergency occurrence.
